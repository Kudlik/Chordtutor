7 chord

The triad of this chord is C Major. Turn off Triad only to see the extra note.

What it is

A major chord with one extra note on top: the flattened seventh. It is often called the dominant seventh.

How it sounds

Tense and a little bluesy. It wants to move on, most often to the chord a fifth below it.

How it is built

Steps from root4, 7, 10
Degrees1, 3, 5, b7
